## 2. Who the app is for

The app is for **parents, guardians and coaches**. An adult creates the account. Children take part through a profile that their parent or guardian sets up and supervises. **Children do not have their own accounts, passwords or email addresses in the app.**

## 3. What we collect

| Information | Why we collect it | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **Your email address** | To create your account and send you a 6-digit sign-in code | You |
| **That you agreed** (the date and the version of these terms you accepted) | To record that a parent or guardian agreed | You |
| **A child's first name and last initial** (for example "Mia R.") | To show the child on the Challenge leaderboard | You, when you add an athlete |
| **Challenge activity**: which exercises were ticked, which days were finished and when, whether a day was finished on time, finisher scores, points and streaks | To run the Christmas Strength Challenge and work out scores and rankings | You, as you use the app |
| **A sign-in token** | To keep you signed in for up to 90 days | Created by our server |

Please **do not enter a child's full name**, a photo, a phone number or any other personal details. The app only asks for a first name and last initial, and rejects anything else.

### Stored only on your own phone
- **Milestone notes** you write in "Celebrate progress". These never leave your phone.
- **Which free items you have added** to your library.
- Your **sign-in token**, kept in your phone's secure storage.

## 4. What we do not collect

We do **not** collect or use: your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, advertising identifiers or health data. The app has **no advertising**, **no analytics or tracking tools** and **no crash-reporting tools**. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

## 5. How we use your information

- To create and secure your account and sign you in.
- To run the Challenge: record progress, calculate points, and show the leaderboard.
- To check scores for fairness. Unusually high scores are flagged so an administrator can review or remove them.
- To contact you about your account or the Challenge, for example to tell you if you win.
- To keep the service secure and to fix problems.

We do not sell your information. We do not send marketing emails unless you agree to that separately.

## 6. Who can see it

- **Other app users (the leaderboard).** Signed-in users can see, for each entrant on the leaderboard: the **first name and last initial, points, streak and a badge** if earned. They cannot see anyone's email, profile or day-by-day activity.
- **Our administrators**, to run the Challenge and review flagged scores.
- **Service providers** who help us run the app, under our instructions:
  - **Railway** hosts our server and database.
  - **Resend** delivers our sign-in emails, so it handles your email address and the email we send you.
- **Apple and Google**, through their app stores, handle the download of the app under their own privacy policies. We receive no personal information from them.
- **Where the law requires it**, for example a court order.

## 7. Where your information is stored

Our server is hosted by Railway in the **United States** (San Francisco region). Resend is also based in the United States. So your information may be stored and handled **outside Australia**. We take reasonable steps to make sure these providers protect it.

## 8. How long we keep it

- **Sign-in codes** (kept only as a one-way scrambled value, together with your email) are deleted when they expire, within about an hour. If you never finish signing in, we do not keep your email.
- **Sign-in tokens** are deleted when they expire (90 days) or when you sign out.
- **Your account, your athletes and their Challenge activity** are kept until you delete them, or for [[RETENTION PERIOD, for example 12 months after the Challenge ends]], whichever is first.
- **Backups** of our database may keep deleted information for up to [[BACKUP PERIOD, confirm with your host]].

## 9. Your choices and rights

You can do the following **inside the app**, under the **Account** tab:
- **Remove an athlete.** This deletes that athlete and all of their scores, and removes them from the leaderboard.
- **Delete your account.** This permanently deletes your email, all your athletes and all their scores.
- **Sign out.**

You can also ask us at **[[CONTACT EMAIL]]** to:
- tell you what personal information we hold about you or your child,
- correct it, or
- delete it.

We will reply within [[30]] days.

## 10. Children's privacy

We take children's privacy seriously.

- A **parent or guardian** must create the account and agrees to this policy for their child.
- We collect only a child's **first name and last initial** and their Challenge activity, and only from the parent or guardian.
- There is **no chat, comments, photo sharing or direct contact** between users.
- Parents can delete a child's information at any time (see section 9).
- If you believe a child has given us information without a parent's agreement, contact us and we will delete it.

## 11. Security

We protect information with reasonable measures, including encrypted connections, one-way scrambling of sign-in codes and tokens, rate limits on sign-in attempts, and access controls so one family cannot see another family's information. No system is perfectly secure.

If there is a data breach that is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as the law requires.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If we make a significant change, we will tell you in the app before it applies. The date at the top shows the latest version.

## 13. Questions and complaints

Contact us first at **[[CONTACT EMAIL]]**. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the **Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC)** at www.oaic.gov.au.
