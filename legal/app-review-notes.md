# Notes for the App Store and Google Play reviewers

*Fill in the three `[[ ]]` items, then paste the text below into **App Store Connect → your app → App Review → Notes**, and into Google Play Console's "App access" instructions. Set the same demo email and code in Railway as `REVIEW_EMAIL` and `REVIEW_CODE`.*

---

**Demo account (no email is needed)**
1. Open the app and tap **Challenge**.
2. Enter the email **[[DEMO EMAIL]]** and tap "Email me a code".
3. Enter the code **[[DEMO CODE]]**. (This account uses a fixed code, so no email is sent.)
4. Tick the box saying you are a parent or guardian, then tap "Create account".
5. Add a child, for example "Demo D.", and tap "Enter the competition".

**About the Christmas Strength Challenge**
- It is a free 24-day challenge that runs from 1 to 24 December 2026. Entry is free and **no purchase is necessary**.
- Because it does not open to the public until 1 December, the demo account above sees the Challenge as if the date were **5 December**. You can tick off the exercises on days 1 to 5, tap "Finish day", enter a finisher score, and see the tree, points, streak and leaderboard.
- The demo account has its own leaderboard. Real users never see it.
- A parent or guardian creates the account. Children do not have accounts, and only a first name and last initial are used.

**About purchases**
- This version has **no in-app purchases**. Everything in the app is free. Paid content will be added in a later update using Apple's In-App Purchase.

**Other things you may look for**
- **Delete account:** Account tab → "Delete my account".
- **Report a name:** Challenge → Leaderboard → tap the flag next to any other entry.
- **Competition rules:** Challenge → "How it works" (the full rules are also at the link on the Account tab). The rules state that the competition is not sponsored, endorsed or run by Apple or Google.
- **Privacy policy, terms and support:** Account tab → About.
