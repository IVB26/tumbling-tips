## 1. How to enter

- **Entry is free.** No purchase is necessary, and buying anything does not improve your chances.
- A **parent or guardian** must create an account in the app and add the child as an "athlete". The parent or guardian agrees to these rules for the child.
- Each athlete enters by being added to the app. An account can have up to **6 athletes**.
- Only a **first name and last initial** is used, for example "Mia R.". Do not use a full name.

## 2. The Challenge

- The Challenge runs for **24 days, from 1 December to 24 December 2026**. The app stays open for catch-ups on 25 and 26 December. **It closes at the start of 27 December 2026.**
- **Which day it is** is decided by our server, using **Sydney, Australia time (Australia/Sydney)**, for everyone. Your phone's clock does not matter.
- Each day has a set of exercises. Tick off each exercise, then a parent or guardian **confirms the day is finished** by pressing "Finish day".

**Daily exercises**
- **Every day:** 30 second handstand hold (against a wall, a pike or tuck handstand on a box, chair or couch, or with someone holding the legs), 10 push-ups, 10 V-snaps.
- **Every 2nd day:** 1 minute plank.
- **Every 3rd day:** 10 pike single leg lifts (each side, plus a hold), 10 pike together lifts, 10 straddle single leg lifts (each side, plus a hold), 10 straddle together lifts.
- **Tuesdays and Fridays:** 20 squat jumps.

## 3. Points

| What | Points |
|---|---|
| Each exercise ticked | 10 |
| Finishing the day | +20 |
| Finishing the day **on its own date** | +10 |

- **Streak bonus.** Finishing days on their own date, one after another, builds a streak. Your daily points (the above) are multiplied: **days 1 to 6 of a streak ×1, days 7 to 12 ×2, days 13 to 18 ×3, days 19 to 24 ×4.** Missing a day, or finishing it late, resets the streak.
- **Catching up.** You can finish a missed day later and still score the basic points, but you do not get the on-the-day bonus, the streak multiplier or the finisher.
- **Daily finisher.** After finishing the day, you may do one all-out test, which changes each day: longest handstand hold (1 point per second), most push-ups in 1 minute (2 points per rep), most V-snaps in 1 minute (2 points per rep), and longest plank (1 point every 2 seconds). It can **only be done on its own date**, once. Finisher points are not multiplied.
- **Limits.** The finisher scores the app accepts have a maximum (for example 600 seconds for a handstand hold, 150 reps in a minute, 1,800 seconds for a plank).

## 4. Winners

- Winners are the entrants with the **highest total points** when the Challenge closes.
- **Ties** go to the entrant with the **higher total finisher points**. If still tied, the entrant who entered **first** wins.
- Winners will be **announced on 27 December 2026** and contacted by **email at the address on their account**. If a winner does not respond within [[CLAIM PERIOD, for example 14 days]], the prize may go to the next entrant.

## 7. Safety

**Take part at your own risk, with adult supervision.** These are strength exercises, including handstands. Warm up first, use a safe, clear space with a soft surface, and stop if anything hurts. If a child has a medical condition or any doubt about whether the exercises are safe, check with a doctor first. Do not attempt advanced skills without a qualified coach.
